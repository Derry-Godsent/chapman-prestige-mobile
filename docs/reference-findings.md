# Reference Findings for Chapman Prestige

## First Recording

The first recording showed a Parcel delivery application. Its appeal came from an uncluttered white canvas, a single illustrated visual focus, large readable form controls, one warm action colour, and clear progression through sign-in. It did not rely on dense cards, gold accents, or decorative icon collections.

## Second Recording

The second recording showed a complete premium-services application. Its visible feature model includes photo-led onboarding with next, back, skip, and progress dots; name-and-phone onboarding followed by a six-digit OTP; a personalised home header and search; active-service progress with a Track Live action; a compact service grid; offers; and a direct booking entry point.

The service flows are specific to the task. Laundry uses item counters. Cleaning and fumigation use property-size estimation with an optional AR room scan. Car detailing uses vehicle and package options. Specialist services offer capacity selection, reviews, and add-ons. The checkout makes payment visible through Mobile Money networks, card options, Apple Pay, promo codes, and a security signal. The app also shows verified workers, availability, assistant and human-support chat, profile controls, a blue-and-white palette, and smooth step transitions.

## Chapman Adaptation

Chapman should adopt the clear command-centre home, service-specific booking forms, transparent payment choice, live service progress, verified worker availability, and direct support. The first build will present AR room scanning, AI chat, live assignment, Apple Pay, and production payment processing as future integrations until the appropriate backend services and provider accounts are configured.
