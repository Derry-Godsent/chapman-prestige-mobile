# Chapman Illustrated Service Model

## Product Promise

Chapman Prestige should explain one service at a time in language that anyone can follow. The customer sees what the service does, what it starts from, what happens next, and how to pay. The app should feel alive through short service illustrations and small movements, not through dense decoration.

## Illustrated Moments

| Service | First illustration | Small motion | Simple customer message |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laundry | A laundry bag fills with neatly folded clothes and leaves on a pickup bike. | Garments drift into the bag; pickup route line draws. | “We pick up, wash, press, and bring your clothes back.” |
| Deep cleaning | A room shifts from untidy to bright and fresh. | Dust particles fade; sunlight moves across the room. | “We clean the parts of your home that take the most time.” |
| Sofa and carpet | Fabric texture changes from dull to clean. | Foam bubbles rise and disappear; colour returns. | “We bring your sofa and carpet back to life.” |
| Fumigation | A shield settles around a home. | A soft perimeter line completes around the building. | “We help protect your home from pests.” |
| Car detailing | A car receives a clean reflective finish. | A light sweep travels across the vehicle. | “We clean and protect your car inside and out.” |
| Polytank | A water tank becomes clear and secure. | A blue water wave rises to a clean line. | “We clean your tank so your water starts safer.” |

## Booking in Four Clear Steps

1. **Choose a service.** The customer sees a clear illustration, a short description, and a starting price.
2. **Tell us about the job.** They choose simple options such as room count, clothing items, tank size, or preferred day.
3. **Choose payment.** The checkout presents mobile money, card, cash where permitted, or pay after quote. The chosen method is visible before confirmation.
4. **Follow the service.** The customer sees a plain status line: Request received → Team confirmed → Specialist assigned → On the way → Work started → Completed.

## Bolt-Like Patterns Adapted for Chapman

The goal is transparency, not imitation. Bolt publicly describes upfront choices, payment methods, scheduled service, and real-time progress as core rider patterns.[1] [2] Chapman should adapt these as clear service estimates, payment selection before confirmation, booking time windows, a live job status timeline, worker details when assigned, and a calm activity history. Bonus value should be visible as a simple earned amount or percentage on the booking confirmation and profile, never as a distracting gold badge.

## Payment and Bonus Rules for the First Build

The interface should show **Mobile Money**, **Card**, **Cash**, and **Pay after quote** as availability depends on the selected service and location. The app must not claim a payment was processed until a real payment provider is integrated. Bonus rewards should appear as a direct message such as “You earned ₵10 Chapman credit” or “5% reward applied”, with the condition written below in smaller text.
