# Chapman Prestige Brand Decisions

The supplied Chapman Prestige Limited logo establishes a **luminous silver water droplet** as the most recognisable compact brand element, supported by a deep midnight-navy field and an electric-blue presentation. The expanded CPL letterform and full legal wordmark are appropriate for the in-app header, but not for a launcher icon where they become unreadable at small sizes.

The revised interface therefore uses the real full logo asset in its home-header treatment, while the launcher uses only the supplied droplet mark on navy. The icon deliberately excludes partial lettering and wordmark fragments to preserve a premium, legible silhouette on iOS and Android launchers.
