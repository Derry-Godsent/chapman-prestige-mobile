## Original supplied logo

The user-supplied `CPL LOGO.png` is the authoritative brand asset. It contains the dark-blue **CPL** monogram, a luminous blue-white water droplet, and the full **CHAPMAN PRESTIGE LIMITED** wordmark on a navy plaque. The mobile app should use this real artwork as the source asset rather than a synthetic droplet/icon replacement. The full business name should be rendered as **Chapman Prestige Limited** in the app header, with the mark appearing naturally beside it instead of inside a generic icon container.
