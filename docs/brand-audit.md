## Website-owned photography audit

The public Netlify page exposes only the Chapman `logo.png` as a reusable image asset. Its dark hero composition does not expose a separate downloadable service photograph in the rendered image list or stylesheet background URLs. Therefore, the mobile service catalogue needs a separately licensed editorial photo set. The intended mobile placement is a restrained service-image strip at the top of selected service detail pages and a single feature photograph in the catalogue introduction, not repeated image-heavy cards.

## Original supplied logo

The user-supplied `CPL LOGO.png` is the authoritative brand asset. It contains the dark-blue **CPL** monogram, a luminous blue-white water droplet, and the full **CHAPMAN PRESTIGE LIMITED** wordmark on a navy plaque. The mobile app should use this real artwork as the source asset rather than a synthetic droplet/icon replacement. The full business name should be rendered as **Chapman Prestige Limited** in the app header, with the mark appearing naturally beside it instead of inside a generic icon container.
