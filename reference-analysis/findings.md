## Accessible Recording

The accessible recording shows a **Parcel** delivery application within an iPhone simulator. Its visible sequence starts with an uncluttered white launch/onboarding moment: a single soft illustration of a delivery rider and parcel sits in a large, quiet canvas with no competing modules. The following authentication screen maintains the same design discipline, using a single warm orange action colour, one human-service illustration at the top, a conventional "Welcome Back" heading, simple outlined inputs, a clear primary button, social sign-in options, and short secondary account links.

The key transferable pattern is not the orange colour or delivery imagery; it is the **single-purpose screen composition**. Each state contains one visual anchor, one headline, one main action, and generous negative space. Feature communication relies on illustration and clean progression rather than stacks of styled cards or decorative badges.

## Chapman Translation

Chapman onboarding will use sequential full-screen service moments with one large image or illustration, concise problem-to-outcome copy, and an unambiguous next action. Sign-in will be reduced to a similarly clear, bottom-anchored action structure. Feature presentation will use visual hierarchy and motion rather than repetitive outlined containers, gold accents, em-dash-heavy copy, or over-decorated icons.
